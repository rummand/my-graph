REGULATION (EU) 2099/42 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL

of 3 March 2099

on the safe keeping of household keys (Key Safety Regulation)

Whereas:

(1)

Keys are often lost.

(2)

Common rules are needed.

HAVE ADOPTED THIS REGULATION:

CHAPTER I

General provisions

Article 1

Subject-matter

This Regulation lays down rules on keeping keys.

Article 2

Definitions

For the purposes of this Regulation:

(1)

‘key’ means any device that opens a lock;

(2)

‘keeper’ means the person who holds a key:

(a)

for a household;

CHAPTER II

Obligations

Article 3

Keeping keys

1. A keeper shall keep keys in a locked place.

2. A keeper shall not lend a key to a stranger, unless:

(a)

the owner agrees; or

(b)

there is an emergency, as referred to in Article 4(1).

Article 4

Reporting

1. A lost key shall be reported within 72 hours.

2. Infringements of Article 3 shall be subject to administrative fines.

Article 5

Entry into force

This Regulation shall enter into force on the twentieth day following that of its publication.
